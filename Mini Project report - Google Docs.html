The project focuses on designing a regulated power supply for a rain-sensing automatic wiper system. The system includes a rain sensor, microcontroller, and wiper motor, all of which require a stable and reliable power source. The power supply converts a 12 V DC input from a battery or adapter into 3.3 V or 5 V regulated outputs, ensuring proper operation of the microcontroller and sensors. It is designed to handle load variations caused by the motor and other components while minimizing voltage ripple and switching noise. Additionally, the supply supports modular powering, allowing independent operation of sensors, MCU, and actuators, ensuring efficient, safe, and reliable performance of the automatic wiper system.
